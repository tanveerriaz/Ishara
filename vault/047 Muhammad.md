## Sample (ayah 1)

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.4rem; text-align: right;">

 ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا۟ وَصَدُّوا۟ عَن سَبِيلِ ٱللَّهِ أَضَلَّ أَعْمَـٰلَهُمْ

</div>

**Sahih International:** Those who disbelieve and avert [people] from the way of Allāh - He will waste their deeds.

**Yusuf Ali:** Those who reject Allah and hinder (men) from the Path of Allah,- their deeds will Allah render astray (from their mark).
