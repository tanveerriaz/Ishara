#### 19:65 · [[019 Maryam]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

رَّبُّ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا فَٱعْبُدْهُ وَٱصْطَبِرْ لِعِبَـٰدَتِهِۦ ۚ هَلْ تَعْلَمُ لَهُۥ سَمِيًّا

</div>

**Word in this verse:** `وَٱصْطَبِرْ` — and be constant

**English (Sahih International):** Lord of the heavens and the earth and whatever is between them - so worship Him and have patience for His worship. Do you know of any similarity to Him?"

**English (Yusuf Ali):** "Lord of the heavens and of the earth, and of all that is between them; so worship Him, and be constant and patient in His worship: knowest thou of any who is worthy of the same Name as He?"

#### 20:132 · [[020 Taha]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

وَأْمُرْ أَهْلَكَ بِٱلصَّلَوٰةِ وَٱصْطَبِرْ عَلَيْهَا ۖ لَا نَسْـَٔلُكَ رِزْقًا ۖ نَّحْنُ نَرْزُقُكَ ۗ وَٱلْعَـٰقِبَةُ لِلتَّقْوَىٰ

</div>

**Word in this verse:** `وَٱصْطَبِرْ` — and be steadfast

**English (Sahih International):** And enjoin prayer upon your family [and people] and be steadfast therein. We ask you not for provision; We provide for you, and the [best] outcome is for [those of] righteousness.

**English (Yusuf Ali):** Enjoin prayer on thy people, and be constant therein. We ask thee not to provide sustenance: We provide it for thee. But the (fruit of) the Hereafter is for righteousness.
