#### 50:3 · [[050 Qaf]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

أَءِذَا مِتْنَا وَكُنَّا تُرَابًا ۖ ذَٰلِكَ رَجْعٌۢ بَعِيدٌ

</div>

**Word in this verse:** `رَجْعٌۢ` — (is) a return

**English (Sahih International):** When we have died and have become dust, [we will return to life]? That is a distant [i.e., unlikely] return."

**English (Yusuf Ali):** "What! When we die and become dust, (shall we live again?) That is a (sort of) return far (from our understanding)."

**Urdu (Fatah Muhammad Jalandhari):** بھلا جب ہم مر گئے اور مٹی ہوگئے (تو پھر زندہ ہوں گے؟) یہ زندہ ہونا (عقل سے) بعید ہے
