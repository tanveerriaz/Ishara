#### 11:98 · [[011 Hud]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

يَقْدُمُ قَوْمَهُۥ يَوْمَ ٱلْقِيَـٰمَةِ فَأَوْرَدَهُمُ ٱلنَّارَ ۖ وَبِئْسَ ٱلْوِرْدُ ٱلْمَوْرُودُ

**Word in this verse:** `يَقْدُمُ` — He will precede

**English (Sahih International):** He will precede his people on the Day of Resurrection and lead them into the Fire; and wretched is the place to which they are led.

**English (Yusuf Ali):** He will go before his people on the Day of Judgment, and lead them into the Fire (as cattle are led to water): But woeful indeed will be the place to which they are led!

**Urdu (Fatah Muhammad Jalandhari):** وہ قیامت کے دن اپنی قوم کے آگے آگے چلے گا اور ان کو دوزخ میں جا اُتارے گا اور جس مقام پر وہ اُتارے جائیں گے وہ برا ہے

#### 25:23 · [[025 Al-Furqan]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

وَقَدِمْنَآ إِلَىٰ مَا عَمِلُوا۟ مِنْ عَمَلٍ فَجَعَلْنَـٰهُ هَبَآءً مَّنثُورًا

</div>

**Word in this verse:** `وَقَدِمْنَآ` — And We will proceed

**English (Sahih International):** And We will approach [i.e., regard] what they have done of deeds and make them as dust dispersed.

**English (Yusuf Ali):** And We shall turn to whatever deeds they did (in this life), and We shall make such deeds as floating dust scattered about.

**Urdu (Fatah Muhammad Jalandhari):** اور جو انہوں نے عمل کئے ہوں گے ہم ان کی طرف متوجہ ہوں گے تو ان کو اُڑتی خاک کردیں گے
