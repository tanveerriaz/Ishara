#### 7:115 · [[007 Al-Araf]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

قَالُوا۟ يَـٰمُوسَىٰٓ إِمَّآ أَن تُلْقِىَ وَإِمَّآ أَن نَّكُونَ نَحْنُ ٱلْمُلْقِينَ

**Word in this verse:** `ٱلْمُلْقِينَ` — the ones to throw

**English (Sahih International):** They said, "O Moses, either you throw [your staff], or we will be the ones to throw [first]."
