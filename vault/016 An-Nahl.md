## Sample (ayah 1)

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.4rem; text-align: right;">

 أَتَىٰٓ أَمْرُ ٱللَّهِ فَلَا تَسْتَعْجِلُوهُ ۚ سُبْحَـٰنَهُۥ وَتَعَـٰلَىٰ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ

</div>

**Sahih International:** The command of Allāh is coming, so be not impatient for it. Exalted is He and high above what they associate with Him.

**Yusuf Ali:** (Inevitable) cometh (to pass) the Command of Allah: seek ye not then to hasten it: Glory to Him, and far is He above having the partners they ascribe unto Him!
