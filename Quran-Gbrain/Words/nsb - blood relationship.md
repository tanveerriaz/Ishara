#### 23:101 · [[023 Al-Muminun]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

فَإِذَا نُفِخَ فِى ٱلصُّورِ فَلَآ أَنسَابَ بَيْنَهُمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ وَلَا يَتَسَآءَلُونَ

#### 25:54 · [[025 Al-Furqan]]

<div dir="rtl" style="font-size: 1.35rem; line-height: 1.9; text-align: right;">

وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ مِنَ ٱلْمَآءِ بَشَرًا فَجَعَلَهُۥ نَسَبًا وَصِهْرًا ۗ وَكَانَ رَبُّكَ قَدِيرًا

**Word in this verse:** `نَسَبًۭا` — blood relationship

**English (Sahih International):** And it is He who has created from water [i.e., semen] a human being and made him [a relative by] lineage and marriage. And ever is your Lord competent [concerning creation].
